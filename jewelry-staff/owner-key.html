Keep the keys under your control

This page creates a server encryption key in your browser and downloads a backup protected by your passphrase. It does not upload the key. Never put the key or passphrase in a chat.

First-time setup only. Replacing an existing keyring without retaining its older keys can prevent existing secure tags from verifying.

In the Hands of Gold hosting project's Production environment, add a Secret named NFC_KEYRING_JSON and paste the copied value. Then publish through the guarded website publisher. Do not replace an existing value without a key-rotation plan.

Keep the encrypted backup on an offline copy in the store safe. Keep the backup passphrase separately; it cannot be recovered from the encrypted file. Test restoration before enrolling the first customer tag.
